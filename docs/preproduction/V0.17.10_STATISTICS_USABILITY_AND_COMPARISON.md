# Smash & Lob v0.17.10 - Statistics usability and richer comparisons

Date: 2026-07-27

## Scope

This release removes redundant information from the statistics workspace, clarifies invitation actions and makes comparison, records and season-summary screens easier to understand.

## Changes

- Uses the classic share symbol for spectator links and a dedicated user-plus symbol for inviting league participants.
- Removes the provisional podium, progress percentage and technical result counters from the statistics landing page.
- Replaces them with leader/champion, most wins, best game differential and best winning streak.
- Removes redundant right-side badges from statistics navigation and aligns the icon, text and chevron columns symmetrically.
- Keeps player selectors sticky and editable while scrolling in Head-to-head and Individual analysis.
- Expands Head-to-head with position, points, win rate, record, game differential, recent form, direct wins, sets, games and performance against common opponents.
- Presents season and player records as plain-language highlights with protagonists, score, round and an explanation of why each match is notable.
- Removes the separate data-status panel from the final season summary.
- Shows an incomplete-data badge beside the season name and disables sharing/downloading until pending, excluded or invalid results are resolved.
- Rebuilds the shareable season image around clear podium and highlight cards.
- Adds cumulative game differential to the league-evolution chart alongside position and points.

## Compatibility

- No Supabase migration.
- No API contract change.
- No permission change.
- No persisted match, ranking or season mutation.
- Existing statistics routes and URL season selection remain compatible.

## Manual validation

1. Confirm the two floating invitation buttons use distinct spectator-share and participant-invite icons.
2. Review the statistics landing page in active and finished seasons, including pending, excluded and invalid results.
3. Scroll Head-to-head and Individual analysis far enough to verify the selectors remain visible and editable without covering essential controls.
4. Compare two players with and without direct matches and with zero, one and several common opponents.
5. Review season and individual records with ties in total games, one-game margins, remontadas and missing records.
6. Open a finished summary with incomplete data and confirm both image actions remain disabled; then resolve the data and confirm sharing/downloading works.
7. Switch league evolution between Position, Points and Game differential, and between All and Top 3.

## Validation gates

Run, in order:

1. `npm run lint`
2. `npx tsc --noEmit`
3. `npm run build`

The review archive excludes `node_modules`, so the full project gates must be executed in the local staging worktree before commit. Focused TypeScript transpilation and structural checks are recorded in the release status.
