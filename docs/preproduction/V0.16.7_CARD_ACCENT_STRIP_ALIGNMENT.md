# v0.16.7 - Card accent strip alignment

Date: 2026-07-26

## Scope

- Correct the Colorful-mode accent strip that could appear detached from or outside some rounded cards, especially on Home.
- Preserve the correctly rounded visual treatment across cards with different padding and content.
- Avoid global overflow clipping so menus, overlays and interactive content remain unaffected.

## Implementation

- Removed the absolutely positioned `app-card::before` accent strip.
- Added the accent gradient as the first background layer of `app-card`.
- Clipped and positioned that layer against the card padding box, so the card border and border radius define its exact geometry.
- Preserved notification, activity and schedule variants through `--app-card-accent-gradient`.

## Validation checklist

1. Open Home in every Colorful palette, both light and dark.
2. Check ranking, next-match, last-match and generic cards at the left and right rounded corners.
3. Confirm the accent strip never appears above or outside the card border.
4. Confirm Settings cards retain their rounded accent treatment.
5. Check notification, activity and schedule cards keep their dedicated gradients.
6. Confirm cards containing links, menus or interactive content are not clipped.
7. Confirm Plain style remains unchanged.

## Persistence and backend

No database migration, API contract, permission or remote persistence change is required.
