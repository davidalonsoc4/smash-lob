# DEMO Scope

## Implemented

- One male compact body and pose, with right/left orientation.
- Two skin tones.
- Bald, messy-short and short-up hair; two colours.
- No beard, short-full beard and goatee; two colours.
- Vertical eyes with two colours.
- Two eyebrow shapes and colours.
- Backwards cap and basic headband with exclusions.
- Two shirt and shorts colour families.
- Optional dominant compression sleeve.
- Optional dominant/non-dominant wristband.
- High/short socks and two shoe combinations.
- Round B racket and diamond stripe racket.
- Randomize, reset, local save and debug recipe.

## Not implemented

Supabase persistence, real profile integration, navigation entry, league screens, exports, Chibi Illustrated visuals, production access or a final large catalogue.
