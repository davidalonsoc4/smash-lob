# Compatibility Rules

Rules are enforced by controls and again by recipe normalization/validation.

1. Cap and headband are mutually exclusive.
2. `hair.short_up_01` is incompatible with `cap.backwards_01` in DEMO 0.1.
3. Selecting a headband turns the cap off.
4. Selecting a cap turns the headband off.
5. The compression sleeve defaults to the dominant arm.
6. Wristband placement is dominant-relative, never screen-relative.
7. Handedness changes racket hand, pose bias, sleeve side and wristband side together.
8. Racket letters are redrawn outside mirrored geometry and remain readable.
9. The racket cord follows racket orientation.
10. Every layer uses the same master anchors.
